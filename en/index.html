Expertise

The micron when it matters, not as a habit

Tight tolerancing is expensive to manufacture. Knowing where it really matters, and where it doesn't, is what keeps a project on budget.

01Mechanical designParts, assemblies, weldments, sheet metal

SolidWorks parts and assemblies, from sketch to production-ready parametric model: mechanisms, welded structures, folded sheet metal.

02GD&T drawingsStandard drawings, functional tolerancing

ISO-standard detail drawings, GD&T tolerancing and bills of materials, ready for your suppliers. Your 2D drawings rebuilt as 3D models.

I size parts for the clearance actually needed, calculate tolerance stacks upfront, and only tolerance tightly where it changes how the assembly behaves. Everything else stays at standard tolerance: it shows directly on the machining quote.

03DFM optimizationThe right process, the right grade

Material grade chosen for the real application: mechanical properties, heat treatment, machinability, cost. A part nitrided to 700 HV does not have the same specification as a black-oxide part.

Machining, sheet metal, welding, fabrication: depending on the part, I point to the right process, which avoids over-specifying a part that should simply have been bent or welded.

04Reverse engineeringScan, STL or undocumented part → STEP

An STL or a point cloud is not CAD: it is a triangulated skin that no automatic converter turns into a machinable part. I rebuild the geometry by hand (surfaces, axes, fillets, holes) to deliver a solid, dimensioned, editable STEP.

Obsolete part, undocumented existing part, designer model to make manufacturable: the deliverable is a file the machinist opens without complaining.

05Design for 3D printingPrint-ready models

Wall thicknesses, print orientation, supports, assembly clearances and tolerances suited to the process. I deliver the print-ready model (STEP or STL) for a prototype, a short run or a jig: printing is done by you or your supplier.

06Tooling and jigsFixtures, inspection gauges

Design of machine tooling and inspection gauges, with a real production mindset: accessibility, part holding, inspection.

07Renders and 3D viewsKeyShot, GLB for online stores

Photorealistic KeyShot renders for product presentations, and interactive 3D views (GLB file) to embed in your online store: rotate, zoom, switch material.
